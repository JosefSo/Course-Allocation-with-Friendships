Slide size:
- 1280x720.

Background:
- Warm paper background #F7F3EA for academic proposal tone.
- Deep ink #17212E for cover, proof panels, and final limitation slide.

Typography:
- Arial for broad compatibility.
- Aptos Mono for formula/code-style labels.
- Titles 32-46px, body 12-18px, labels 9-12px, footer 8.5px.

Palette:
- Ink: #17212E.
- Paper: #F7F3EA.
- Muted gray: #56616F.
- Teal: #0F766E for social mechanism and primary contribution.
- Berry: #9F1239 for trade-offs and risk.
- Gold: #B7791F for evidence and hypothesis markers.
- Blue: #355070 for method and analysis.

Chart grammar:
- Direct labels, no heavy legends.
- Baseline in muted gray, key intervention in teal, negative/trade-off in berry.
- Every numeric claim carries a source caption.

Diagram grammar:
- Left-to-right reading order.
- Arrows only where direction or causal logic matters.
- Boxes encode constructs, variables, stages, or grouped metrics.

Footer grammar:
- Thin rule above footer.
- Source note on left; slide number on right.
