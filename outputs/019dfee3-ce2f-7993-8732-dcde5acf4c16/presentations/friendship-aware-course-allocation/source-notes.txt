Sources used:

1. README.md
- Project title: HBS Social: Course Allocation with Friendships.
- Model: deterministic snake-draft HBS-style course allocation with reactive social preferences.
- Inputs: Table 1 individual course preferences, Table 2 directed friend preferences by course, Table 3 per-student LambdaFriend.
- Utility: U(s,c) = (1 - lambda_s) Base(s,c) + lambda_s FriendBonusNorm(s,c).
- Reactive friend bonus counts only friends already allocated to the candidate course.
- Post-allocation stage includes swap, drop-add, and hybrid modes under global or personal objectives.
- Metrics: total utility, normalized Gini, Jain, Theil, Atkinson, percentiles, friendship-overlap statistics, position statistics, fill rate.

2. course_allocation_abstract_review.txt
- Recommended research framing: course allocation is a one-sided combinatorial assignment problem with indivisible course seats, capacities, and no real monetary transfers.
- Research gap: standard course allocation models usually treat student preferences as independent and ignore friendship relations.
- Contribution language: friendship-aware extension with directed friendship-based social term and post-allocation local-search improvements.

3. results/experiments/experiments.sqlite
- Saved aggregate experiment evidence for scenario A_200x8.
- Scenario: 200 students, 8 courses, capacity 80, b=3, draft_rounds=3, seeds 11-20.
- Saved labels include legacy aliases:
  adaptive-global = current hybrid-global.
  adaptive-greedy = current hybrid-personal.
  add-drop = current drop-add-global.
  swap = current swap-global.
- Baseline no-post mean total utility: 371.310.
- Hybrid-global at post_iters=5: mean total utility 384.805, +3.63%.
- Swap-global at post_iters=20: mean total utility 379.516, +2.21%.
- Drop-add-global at post_iters=20: mean total utility 372.275, +0.26%.
- Hybrid-personal at post_iters=20: mean total utility 369.862, -0.39%.
- Friend overlap per student: 3.715 baseline -> 4.697 hybrid-global at post_iters=5, +26.44%.
- Average assigned position: 2.186 baseline -> 2.434 hybrid-global at post_iters=5.
- Share top-3 assigned courses: 0.872 baseline -> 0.796 hybrid-global at post_iters=5.
- Gini total norm: 0.05238 baseline -> 0.05576 hybrid-global at post_iters=5.
