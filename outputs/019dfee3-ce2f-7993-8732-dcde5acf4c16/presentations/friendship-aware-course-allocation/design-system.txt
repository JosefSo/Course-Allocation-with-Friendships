Slide size: 1280x720.

Background system:
- Warm academic paper background (#F7F3EA) for most slides.
- Deep ink panels (#17212E) for emphasis and final synthesis.
- Hairline rules instead of decorative card grids.

Typography:
- Claim titles: Aptos Display, large, sentence-case, academic conclusion.
- Labels and body: Aptos, compact and readable.
- Formula fragments: Aptos Mono only when useful.

Palette:
- Ink: #17212E.
- Muted text: #56616F.
- Paper: #F7F3EA.
- Rule: #D7CDBE.
- Teal accent: #0F766E.
- Berry accent: #9F1239.
- Gold accent: #B7791F.
- Blue-gray accent: #355070.

Chart grammar:
- Direct labels and annotated deltas.
- Bars and matrices preferred over thin line charts.
- Values are drawn from local experiment sources and footnoted quietly.

Diagram grammar:
- Equal-role boxes share dimensions and typography.
- Directional edges are used only when direction matters.
- Reactive friendship logic is represented as sequence/order, not as a static undirected graph.

Footer/page marker:
- Bottom-left source note; bottom-right slide number.
- Sources are local project files rather than external citations.

Allowed layout families:
- Cover with contribution rail.
- Problem triad.
- Technical system map.
- Formula proof slide.
- Sequential mechanism diagram.
- Method matrix.
- Experiment pipeline.
- Bar-chart result slide.
- Trade-off dashboard.
- Synthesis / research agenda.

Banned motifs:
- Generic rounded SaaS cards.
- Marketing hero layout.
- Decorative logos or pseudo-institution marks.
- Dense code screenshots.
