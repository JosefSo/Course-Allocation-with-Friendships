Slide size:
- 1280x720.

Background:
- Warm paper background #F7F3EA for research-proposal tone.
- Deep ink #17212E for cover, section callouts, and proof panels.

Typography:
- Hebrew body and titles use Arial for broad Hebrew support.
- English formulas and code-style labels use Aptos Mono when available.
- Titles 32-46px, body 14-19px, labels 9-12px, footer 8.5px.

Palette:
- Ink: #17212E.
- Paper: #F7F3EA.
- Muted gray: #56616F.
- Teal: #0F766E for social mechanism / primary contribution.
- Berry: #9F1239 for trade-off / risk.
- Gold: #B7791F for evidence and hypothesis markers.
- Blue: #355070 for methodology and analysis.

Chart grammar:
- Direct labels, no heavy legends.
- Baseline in muted gray, key intervention in teal, negative/trade-off in berry.
- Numerical results must carry scenario/source captions.

Diagram grammar:
- Right-to-left reading order for Hebrew conceptual slides.
- Arrows only where direction or causal logic matters.
- Boxes encode actual constructs, variables, stages, or grouped metrics.

Footer grammar:
- Thin rule above footer.
- Source note on left; slide number on right.

Allowed layouts:
- Cover with dark proof rail.
- Research gap triad.
- Literature map.
- RQ/H matrix.
- Conceptual model diagram.
- Factorial design table.
- Measurement/validity matrix.
- Analysis pipeline.
- Bar proof slide.
- Trade-off dashboard.
- Contribution grid.
- Limitations/robustness plan.

Banned motifs:
- Generic card grid repeated more than twice.
- Decorative blobs, pseudo-logos, mascot/icons, unrelated stock imagery.
- Unlabeled diagrams or charts without source note.
